Executive Brief: SIH-26037 Autonomous Driving System

MathWorks Smart Vehicles | Unstructured Indian Road Ecosystem

1. Executive Summary & Problem Context

Standard Western Autonomous Driving (AD) stacks rely heavily on geometric cues (clean painted lanes, strict lane discipline, standardized signs, predictable vehicular actors). On unstructured Indian roadways, these assumptions catastrophically fail due to:

  • Heterogeneous Mixed Traffic: Auto-rickshaws, two-wheelers, stray cattle, and pushcart vendors with starkly contrasting dynamics.
  • Unstructured Infrastructure: Missing lane markings, unpaved dirt shoulders, potholes, and informal merging.
  • Dynamic Deadlocks: High crowd density causes traditional planners to suffer from the Freezing Robot Problem.

2. MATLAB & Simulink Mathematical Formulations

This software stack provides closed-loop algorithmic rigor developed and validated in MATLAB & Simulink:

1. Stanley Lateral Steering Control Law:
δ(t) = (ψref - ψ) + arctan( -k · efa(t) / (v(t) + ε) )
Gain k = 1.8, Softening speed ε = 1.0 m/s, Max steer = ±35°

Auto-Rickshaw: ac = 4.0m, bc = 1.8m | Two-Wheeler: ac = 3.8m, bc = 1.6m
